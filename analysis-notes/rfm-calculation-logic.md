# RFM Score Calculation Logic

##  Purpose
RFM scoring converts customer behavior into a structured segmentation framework that supports retention and profitability analysis.

The scoring process transforms raw customer metrics into comparable rankings.

---

##  Step 1: Recency Score
Customers are ranked based on how recently they made a purchase.

More recent activity → higher score.

This helps identify:
- Active customers
- Dormant customers
- Churn risk segments

---

##  Step 2: Frequency Score
Customers are ranked based on how often they purchase.

Higher purchase frequency → higher score.

This highlights loyal and repeat customers.

---

##  Step 3: Monetary Score
Customers are ranked based on total revenue contribution.

Higher spending → higher score.

This identifies high-value customers.

---

##  Final RFM Segment
The three scores are combined to classify customers into segments such as:
- High-value loyal customers
- At-risk customers
- Low-engagement customers

This allows targeted retention strategies instead of generic campaigns.

---

##  Analyst Note
RFM scoring simplifies complex customer behavior into actionable business segments that support retention and profitability decisions.
